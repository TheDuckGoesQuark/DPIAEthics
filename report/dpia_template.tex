It follows the process set out in our DPIA guidance, and should be read alongside that guidance and the \href{http://ec.europa.eu/newsroom/document.cfm?doc_id=47711}{Criteria for an acceptable DPIA} set out in European guidelines on DPIAs. \par


\vspace{\baselineskip}
